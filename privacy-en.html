Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

In short

Pecule does not collect any personal data. There is no account to create, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Your data stays on your Mac

Your accounts, transactions, categories, schedules, attachments and backups are stored only on your Mac, in the files you choose. They are not sent to any server and we have no access to them.

Imports (CSV, OFX, QIF, PDF statements) and exports (CSV, PDF, printing) only happen when you ask for them, from and to the files you select.

Network connections

The app makes only one network connection: the Stock Market tool queries Yahoo Finance to show the prices of the securities you follow. Only the security's symbol (for example “AAPL”) is sent; none of your account or transaction data. This service is subject to Yahoo's privacy policy.

If you don't use the Stock Market tool, Pecule does not connect to the Internet.

Sharing

No data is shared, sold or passed on to third parties.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may record technical connection information (such as your IP address) under its own privacy policy.

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and the download counter reads the public release statistics on GitHub.

Your language choice and the counter's last total are kept in your browser's local storage. The site uses no cookies and no analytics.

Changes

This page may change; the date of the last update is shown at the top.
